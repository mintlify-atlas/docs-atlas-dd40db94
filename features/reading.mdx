Koodo Reader provides a full-featured reading view with multiple layout options, precise navigation, and a clean reader menu that keeps controls out of the way until you need them.

## Opening a book

Click or tap any book in the library to open it. By default, the reader opens in a separate window on desktop. To open books in the main window instead, enable **Open books in the main window** in Settings → Reading.

<Tip>
Enable **Auto open last-read book** in Settings → Reading to automatically resume where you left off each time Koodo Reader launches.
</Tip>

On the desktop app, you can also configure books to open maximized or in full screen automatically via **Auto open book in maximized screen** and **Auto open book in full screen** in Settings → Reading.

## Reading layouts

Koodo Reader supports three layout modes for reflowable formats (EPUB, TXT, FB2, DOCX, etc.):

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Single-column" icon="rectangle-vertical">
    One column of text fills the reading area. The most common layout, suited to most screen sizes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Two-column" icon="columns-2">
    Content is split into two side-by-side columns, resembling a physical book spread. Well-suited to large displays in landscape orientation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Continuous scroll" icon="arrow-down">
    Text flows vertically without page breaks. Scroll through the entire book like a web page.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
PDF files use a fixed-page layout and do not support reflowable column modes. PDF-specific options include rendering from an even page, page scaling, and color inversion.
</Note>

### Additional layout options

The following toggles are available in the reader settings panel:

| Option | Description |
|---|---|
| Sliding animation | Animated page transitions |
| Fast reading mode (Bionic) | Makes the first half of each word bold to guide the eye |
| Text indentation | Adds paragraph indentation |
| Enable hyphenation | Hyphenates long words at line breaks |
| Bold / Italic / Underline / Shadow | Text style overrides |
| Invert color | Inverts the rendering for dark-background reading |
| Show page border | Displays a visible border around the page |

## Navigation

### Page turning

- **Click** the left or right edge of the reading area to turn pages.
- **Arrow keys** (`←` / `→`) move backward and forward one page.
- **Space** or **Page Down** advances one page; **Shift+Space** or **Page Up** goes back.

<Tip>
In continuous scroll mode, use the scroll wheel or trackpad to move through the text instead of clicking page zones.
</Tip>

### Table of contents

Open the reader menu (hover or click the top area of the reader) and select the **Contents** panel to see the full table of contents. Click any chapter title to jump directly to it.

### Progress tracking

The footer bar displays your current reading progress as a percentage, derived from the CFI (Canonical Fragment Identifier) position within the book. You can also drag the progress slider in the footer to jump to any point in the book.

The header shows the current chapter title as you read.

<Note>
Header and footer display can be hidden individually via **Hide header** and **Hide footer** in the reader settings panel.
</Note>

### Auto-scroll between chapters

When you reach the last page of a chapter in paginated mode, Koodo Reader automatically advances to the next chapter. To disable this behavior, enable **Disable auto scroll to next or previous chapter** in Settings → Reading.

## Text selection

Selecting text in the reader opens a popup toolbar with quick actions: highlight, add a note, translate, look up in dictionary, copy, and search.

<Note>
If you are using a third-party translation service that needs access to selected text, enable **No popup when selecting texts** in Settings → Reading. With this setting on, Koodo's popup will not appear on selection; right-clicking the selection will restore it.
</Note>

The popup can also be suppressed by enabling **Prevent accidental trigger**, which requires a click on the reader menu area rather than a hover to reveal controls.

## Touch screen support

Enable **Touch screen mode** in Settings → Reading to activate gesture and UI optimizations for touch devices:

- Swipe left/right to turn pages.
- Tap the center of the screen to show or hide the reader menu.
- Pinch to zoom on PDF pages.

This mode is automatically recommended on Android and iOS, but can also be enabled on desktop touch displays.

## Text-to-speech

Koodo Reader includes a text-to-speech (TTS) feature accessible from the reader menu via the audiobook button. TTS reads the current book aloud, advancing through chapters automatically.

TTS voices include device voices (available offline) and AI-powered voices via the Koodo cloud service. AI voices require a Koodo account and are subject to a daily character quota.

For configuration details — including voice selection, speed, and pitch — see [Plugins](/customization/plugins).

<Note>
The audiobook/TTS button can be hidden using **Hide audiobook button** in the reader settings panel if you do not use this feature.
</Note>

## Reader menu

Hover over (or click, in prevent-trigger mode) the top edge of the reader to reveal the menu bar. It provides access to:

- Table of contents
- Bookmarks list
- Annotations list
- Reading settings (font, layout, background, margins)
- AI tools panel
- Progress slider
- Page scale controls (PDF)

<Tip>
On the desktop app, enable **Merge reader into Word** in Settings → Reading to hide the reader window frame and blend it into a text editor, useful for discreet reading alongside work.
</Tip>
