Koodo Reader includes a set of AI-powered tools accessible from the reader view. These features use Koodo's cloud service and require a Koodo account to function.

<Note>
AI features can be disabled entirely by enabling **Disable AI features** in Settings → General. Individual buttons in the reader menu can also be hidden — see **Hide AI button** in the reader settings panel.
</Note>

## Accessing AI tools

AI tools are available from two entry points:

1. **Text selection popup** — select any text in the reading view to open the popup toolbar, which includes quick-access buttons for dictionary lookup and translation.
2. **AI panel** — click the AI button in the reader menu bar to open the full AI panel, which provides summarization, Q&A, and chat modes.

## AI dictionary

The dictionary looks up a selected word and returns a structured definition powered by AI. The response includes:

- Pronunciation (with phonetic notation)
- Audio playback (when available)
- Word inflections
- Definitions grouped by part of speech, each with example sentences and translations
- Word comparison analysis for similar or commonly confused words

### Looking up a word

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a word">
    Double-click a word, or click and drag to select it in the reader.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the dictionary icon">
    In the selection popup, click the dictionary (book) icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View the definition">
    The AI dictionary panel opens with the full entry, including pronunciations, meanings, and examples.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Supported dictionary languages

The dictionary supports automatic language detection plus explicit source languages including English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Classical Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Russian, and Portuguese.

<Tip>
Dictionary lookup history is saved and can be exported as a CSV file from the export options in the annotations panel.
</Tip>

## AI translation

Translation converts selected text from one language to another using a streaming AI model. The result appears in a panel as the translation is generated in real time.

### Translating selected text

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select text">
    Click and drag to select the passage you want to translate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the translate icon">
    In the selection popup, click the translate icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose target language">
    Select your target language from the language picker. The source language is detected automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the translation">
    The translation streams into the panel in real time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Supported translation languages

Translation supports over 80 target languages, including: English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, Portuguese, German, Italian, Russian, Thai, Vietnamese, Arabic, Hindi, and many more. The full list is available in Settings → Language.

<Note>
Koodo also supports batch translation of multiple text segments via the internal batch translation API, used when translating longer passages or paginated content.
</Note>

## AI summarization

Summarization generates a concise summary of the current chapter's text. It uses the same streaming response model as translation, so results appear progressively.

### Summarizing a chapter

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the AI panel">
    Click the AI button in the reader menu bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Ask mode">
    The panel opens in Ask mode by default, which operates on the current chapter's text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use a sample prompt or type your own">
    Koodo provides sample prompts such as "Summarize this chapter for me" and "What are the key points of this chapter". Click one or type your own question.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the response">
    The AI generates a streamed response based on the chapter content.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## AI chat

In addition to chapter-focused Q&A, the AI panel includes a **Chat** mode for open-ended conversations not tied to the current chapter text. Sample prompts include book recommendations and explanations of literary concepts.

Switch between Ask and Chat modes using the mode toggle at the top of the AI panel.

<Tip>
By default, the AI chat panel auto-scrolls to the bottom as responses stream in. To disable this and scroll manually, enable **Disable automatically scroll to the bottom of the AI chat** in Settings → Reading.
</Tip>

## Account and quota

All AI features — dictionary, translation, summarization, and TTS AI voices — require a Koodo account and communicate with Koodo's cloud service.

| Feature | Account required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| AI dictionary | Yes | Per-request; subject to daily quota |
| AI translation | Yes | Streaming; subject to daily quota |
| AI summarization | Yes | Streaming; subject to daily quota |
| AI TTS voices | Yes | Subject to daily character quota; purchasable quota available for Pro users |

When the daily quota is exhausted, Koodo will prompt you to wait for the quota reset or, for AI TTS, to upgrade to Pro or purchase additional quota.

<Warning>
AI features require an active internet connection and a valid Koodo account session. Offline reading is fully supported, but AI tools will not function without connectivity.
</Warning>

## Plugin dependency

AI features are powered by Koodo's built-in cloud service (`ReaderRequest`), not by local models. No separate AI plugin is required for dictionary, translation, or summarization. However, some extended AI behaviors — such as custom TTS voices or third-party model integrations — are configured via plugins.

For plugin setup and TTS configuration, see [Plugins](/customization/plugins).
